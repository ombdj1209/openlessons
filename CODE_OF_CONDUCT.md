# Code of Conduct

OpenLessons exists to help people learn. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity, and beginners most of all.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

* Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to a topic or to open source
* Critique lessons and code, never people
* Assume good intent, and ask before you assume
* No harassment, insults, or unwelcome attention of any kind, in any project space

## Reporting

If someone makes you or others feel unsafe or unwelcome, email the maintainer at **omprakashbdj1209@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially and promptly.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
